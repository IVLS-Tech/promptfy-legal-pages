1. Accounts and Identifiers

Promptfy creates an anonymous account automatically the first time you open the app, identified by a persistent device identifier stored on your device. This lets you use the app and sync your content without signing in. You may optionally use Sign in with Apple to link this account to your Apple ID so your data can be restored on another device. We store the Apple identifier associated with your account, and your email only if you choose to share it.

2. Content You Create (synced to our backend)

Unlike earlier versions, Promptfy 2.0 syncs your content to our cloud backend so it is available across your devices. This includes:

  • Scripts and creator profiles you create in the app.
  • Voice samples and voice clones, when you use voice cloning — including audio recordings you provide and the resulting synthetic voice.
  • Media you submit for processing (e.g., audio/video sent for text-to-speech, dubbing, background removal, or subtitles).
  • AI-generated and translated content produced from your inputs.

3. Usage, Device, and Diagnostic Data

We collect device details, app version, timestamps, feature usage, and crash/diagnostic data to operate and improve the Service.

4. Subscriptions, Credits, and Transactions

When you purchase a subscription (Starter, Pro, or Studio — monthly or annual — or the legacy Pro plan) or a credit pack, we record the product identifier, transaction identifiers, subscription status, renewal/expiration, and credit balance provided by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Notifications

With your permission, we store push notification tokens to send you notifications (e.g., job completion, subscription and migration reminders). You can disable notifications in your device settings.

  • TikTok Share SDK — only when you choose to share content to TikTok.
